## About KUYAKHANYA FM

KUYAKHANYA FM is a community-focused online Christian radio station based in Thembalethu, George, South Africa. The station was birthed from a God-given vision — through a dream, the call came to bring light to the people of Thembalethu. KUYAKHANYA FM exists to uplift, inspire, and transform lives by spreading the light of Christ, especially to those walking in spiritual darkness.

The station is more than just radio; it is a movement of hope, unity, and empowerment for the community. It uses the power of broadcasting to bring people together, spread the love of God, and empower individuals through uplifting music, inspirational messages, and community-driven programmes.

## Vision

To be the light of hope and transformation in Thembalethu and beyond, shining the love of God through radio broadcasting, inspiring positive change, and uniting the community to walk in truth, purpose, and spiritual growth.

## Weekly Shows & Presenters

KUYAKHANYA FM airs programmes from Tuesday to Sunday.

- **Tuesday — Love & Life** (Host: Franky). Warm conversation around love, life and relationships — real stories, heartfelt advice and community voices that heal, connect and celebrate everyday joy.
- **Wednesday — Abafazi Bokholo** (Host: Mrs. Lolwana). Celebrating women of faith, resilience, wisdom and purpose — powerful testimonies and honest dialogue.
- **Wednesday — Inkwenkwezi Zethu** (Host: Mrs. Klass). Inspiration that connects — stories of empowerment, faith and hope that uplift our community.
- **Thursday — Ithemba Lokulila** (Hosts: Mrs. Mati & Mrs. Panduva). Faith, hope and encouragement for the journey — a compassionate space where community finds healing, prayer and strength.
- **Friday — Friday Vibes** (Host: Kay Cee Taurus). Feel the weekend energy! Music, conversation and vibrant weekend vibes.
- **Saturday — Ezakwa Ntu** (Host: DJ Toto). A proudly African celebration of culture, heritage and roots — music, language and stories.
- **Sunday — Yizani Sakhe — Together We Revive** (Hosts: Mrs. Mthwesi & Mrs. Ndziba). An inspirational Sunday gathering of faith, restoration and revival.
